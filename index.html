The Signature Interior Deep Clean

$180 - $240

Our most popular interior service, designed to bring your car's cabin back to its best possible condition. Tackles built-up dirt, light stains, and odors to leave the interior sanitized.

  • Steam cleaning to sanitize vents & cupholders
  • Shampoo all carpets & cloth seats to lift dirt
  • UV protectant on hard surfaces (dashboards)
  • Specialized cleaner & conditioner for leather
Best For: Cars that have accumulated dirt and grime over time or need more than a basic cleaning.

The Express Interior Tidy

Starting at $125

Designed for vehicles that are already in good condition and just need a quick, professional refresh. Perfect as a maintenance service.

  • Thorough vacuum of all carpets and seats
  • Wipe-down of all hard interior surfaces
  • Cleaning of interior windows
  • Cleaning of door jambs
Best For: Cars that are regularly maintained and just need a professional touch-up.

The Signature Shine & Decon

Starting at $180

Our most requested exterior service, perfect for restoring your car's glossy finish. Removes embedded contaminants that standard washes miss.

  • Includes everything in the Express Wash
  • Clay bar treatment for bonded contaminants
  • Durable paint sealant (4-6 months protection)
Best For: Restoring the shine on your vehicle's paint and providing long-lasting protection.
